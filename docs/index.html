Dental Abscess Treatment in Manchester, MO

A dental abscess is a painful pocket of infection caused by bacteria, usually from deep decay, a cracked tooth or gum disease. It will not go away on its own, and waiting can allow the infection to spread to your jaw, face or beyond.

Our emergency dentists provide prompt dental abscess treatment to relieve pain, clear the infection and protect your smile, day or night.

  • Severe, throbbing or constant toothache
  • Swelling in the gums, cheek or jaw
  • A pimple-like bump on the gum
  • Bad taste, bad breath or sensitivity to hot and cold
Go to the ER or call 911 if you have swelling that is spreading, trouble breathing or swallowing, or a high fever.

FAQs

Dental Abscess & Emergency Care FAQs

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from patients in pain.

What are the signs of a dental abscess?
Common signs include a severe, throbbing toothache, swelling in the gum, cheek or face, a pimple-like bump on the gums, a bad taste in the mouth, sensitivity to hot and cold, and sometimes fever. A dental abscess does not heal on its own, so you should seek treatment as soon as possible.
Can I get same day dental abscess treatment in Manchester, MO?
Yes. We are a 24 hour emergency dentist and prioritize urgent cases such as dental abscesses. Call 636-267-0915 and we will arrange an appointment as quickly as possible, including after hours.
Will I need a root canal or an extraction for an abscessed tooth?
It depends on how much of the tooth can be saved. When the tooth is restorable, an emergency root canal removes the infection and preserves the tooth. If the damage is too severe, an emergency tooth extraction may be the safest option. Your dentist will explain your choices after an exam.
What should I do while waiting for my emergency dental appointment?
Rinse gently with warm salt water, take over-the-counter pain relief as directed on the label, and keep your head elevated. Do not place aspirin on the gum and do not try to drain the abscess yourself. If you have facial swelling that spreads, trouble swallowing or breathing, or a high fever, go to the nearest emergency room or call 911.
Do you offer affordable emergency dental care?
We aim to make urgent dental care accessible. Call 636-267-0915 to ask about fees, payment options and insurance before your visit, so you know what to expect.
